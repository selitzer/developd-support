Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Developd is a shared disposable camera app that lets users create photo rolls, invite friends, take photos together, and view those photos once a roll has developed. This Privacy Policy explains what information Developd collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

Developd may collect and store:

  • Account information used to sign in with Apple.
  • Your display name and profile photo, if you choose to provide them.
  • Photos you take and upload through the app.
  • Information related to your shared rolls, memberships, invites, and photo activity.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only to provide the features of Developd, including account authentication, shared rolls, photo storage, development, and profile features.

Photo Access

Developd uses your device camera so you can take photos within the app. Access to your photo library may also be used when you choose to save photos to your device.

Data Storage

Developd uses third-party infrastructure providers to securely store account data, roll information, and uploaded photos. Data is transmitted using encrypted network connections.

Sharing

Developd does not sell your personal information. Photos and roll information are shared only with users who are members of the same shared roll, as required for the app to function.

Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as needed to provide Developd's features. Data associated with deleted content may also remain temporarily in backups or system logs.

Children's Privacy

Developd is not intended for children under the age required to independently consent to data processing in their region.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Developd changes. Any updates will be posted on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at developdsupport@gmail.com.
